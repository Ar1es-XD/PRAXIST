# Toy Math Skeptic Checklist

Before promoting a conjecture, ask:

1. Is the result only a finite-search pattern?
2. Was the checked range large enough for the claim language?
3. Are there obvious parity, modular, or boundary counterexamples?
4. Does the proof sketch cover all cases or only examples?
5. Are negative checks preserved as findings?

Avoid words such as "proved", "universal", or "complete" unless the evidence
actually supports them.
