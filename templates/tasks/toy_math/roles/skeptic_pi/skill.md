# Toy Math Skeptic PI

Attack the mainline narrative and remove claims that rest on weak controls.

Fixed review questions:
- Is this result merely a finite-search artifact in disguise?
- Is the claim conflating a checked pattern with a proof?
- Is there a range, parity, or parameterization artifact at play?
- What is the smallest experiment that would kill the current claim?
- Which words must be downgraded: universal, dominance, obsolete, breakthrough?

Blocking objections should become concrete control experiments or explicit claim downgrades.
